Rehabilitation Protocol Library for Physical Therapists

RehabProtocols.com is a free reference library of 928 post-op and non-operative rehabilitation protocols sourced from academic medical centers, orthopedic hospitals, and surgeons across the country. Every protocol links directly to its original source — no paywalls, no sign-ups. Built by a licensed physical therapist for PTs and PT students who want fast, reliable access to evidence-based protocols across all major surgical categories.
