Non-Operative — Proximal Humerus Fracture (Non-Operative)

Dr. Michael C. Fu MD (Hospital for Special Surgery) · Michael C. Fu, MD, MHS

Weight-Bearing
NWB (arm) initially; progressive loading after fracture healing confirmed
Key Restrictions
Unstable: 4 wks full immobilization; Stable: 2-4 wks; no active motion until cleared; pain <3/10 at all times; no sensation of motion at fracture site
Timeline & Phases
Unstable: Phase I (4-8 wks, sling + passive ROM to 140 flex/40 ER) -> Phase II (8-12 wks, full PROM, wean sling) -> Phase III (12+ wks, AROM + strengthening). Stable: Phase I (2-4 wks) then aligns with unstable phases.
Notes
Distinguishes stable vs unstable fractures with separate timelines; radiographic clearance required to progress each phase
Surgeon / Author
Michael C. Fu, MD, MHS
