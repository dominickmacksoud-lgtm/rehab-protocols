ACL Reconstruction — All Graft Types

Brigham and Women's Hospital · Dept. of Rehabilitation Services

Weight-Bearing
Per graft type: BPTB WBAT; Hamstring PWB initially; Allograft more conservative; meniscal recon: avoid WB >45 deg flex x 4 wks, limit to 90 deg wks 4-8
Key Restrictions
BPTB: increased anterior knee pain with kneeling; Hamstring: no resisted hamstring x 12 wks; Allograft: slightly increased graft failure risk; locked hinged brace post-op; meniscal recon specific WB/ROM restrictions
Timeline & Phases
Criterion-based phases adopted from Adams et al (2012) JOSPT and Delaware PT Clinic Guidelines; includes hop testing and functional assessment
Notes
Comprehensive protocol covering all graft types; 2015; based on Adams et al JOSPT 2012; hop test criteria: >=80% strength and >=85% functional testing for return to sport; separate from BWH's graft-specific protocols
Surgeon / Author
Dept. of Rehabilitation Services
