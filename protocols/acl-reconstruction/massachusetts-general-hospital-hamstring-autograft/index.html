ACL Reconstruction — Hamstring Autograft

Massachusetts General Hospital · MGH Sports Medicine

Weight-Bearing
PWB with crutches x 6 wks (unless otherwise instructed by MD)
Key Restrictions
Same as BPTB plus: no resisted hamstring strengthening until 12 wks; PWB with crutches x 6 wks; early return to sport phase delayed
Timeline & Phases
6 phases: Phase I (0-2 wks), Phase II (3-5 wks), Phase III (6-8 wks), Phase IV (9-12 wks), Phase V (early return to sport), Phase VI (unrestricted return to sport)
Notes
Same document as BPTB entry; hamstring-specific modifications noted throughout; delayed hamstring loading is key differentiator
Surgeon / Author
MGH Sports Medicine
