Platelet-Rich Plasma — (PRP) Rehabilitation

UW Health Sports Medicine · Ken Krogman, MSPT, ATC; Marc Sherry, PT, DPT, LAT, CSCS; Dr. John Wilson; UW…

Weight-Bearing
Varies by body part: upper extremity sling 0-10 days; lower extremity PWB with crutches ± boot 0-10 days; wean to full WB Phase II
Key Restrictions
Phase I (0-3 days): immobilization — sling for RC/biceps, wrist splint for elbow epicondyles, PWB + crutches for patellar/quad tendons, PWB + crutches + boot for Achilles/ankle/plantar fascia; no overstressing tendon through exercise/lifting/impact until Phase III; avoid high velocity/plyometrics/running/jumping/heavy lifting until Phase IV
Timeline & Phases
Phase I (0-3 days): immobilization per body part, gentle AROM; Phase II (3-10-14 days): wean from device, AROM 3x daily, cross-training opposite extremity; Phase III (14 days to 6-8 wks): full ROM, isometric → concentric → eccentric strengthening, proprioception, non-impact cardio; Phase IV (6-8+ wks): eccentric multi-plane strength, impact control, sport-specific progressions, RTS with physician/PT clearance
Notes
Non-surgical PRP injection for tendinopathy across multiple body regions: rotator cuff, biceps, medial/lateral epicondyle, patellar tendon, quad tendon, Achilles, ankle tendons, plantar fascia. Rehab appointments begin 10-14 days post-injection. Authors: Ken Krogman MSPT ATC, Marc Sherry PT DPT LAT CSCS, Dr. John Wilson. Updated 04/2014. Copyright 2014.
