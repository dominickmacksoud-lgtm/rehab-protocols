Pectoralis Major Repair — Pectoralis Major Tendon Repair

Sutter Health / Palo Alto Medical Foundation · Kevin Roth MD

Weight-Bearing
N/A (upper extremity)
Key Restrictions
Sling with abduction pillow at all times wks 1-6 (remove for exercises/showering); D/C sling at wks 6-8; Phase III AROM only — no PROM, AAROM (no pulleys, supine wand, or wall climb); no flies or pulldowns until 4.5 months; light weight training at 4.5 months post-op; RTS at 6 months
Timeline & Phases
Phase I (wks 1-4): sling with abduction pillow, pendulum exercises under PT guidance, elbow/wrist AROM with shoulder in neutral, hand exercises, stationary bike with arm in sling | Phase II (wks 4-6): sling with abduction pillow, AAROM supine with wand, goal FE to 90° | Phase III (wks 6-8): D/C sling, AROM in pain-free range only (NO PROM, AAROM, pulleys, supine wand, wall climb), goals FE 120°/abduction 90°/ER as tolerated/IR and extension with wand behind back, submaximal isometrics | Phase IV (wks 8-12): AROM and AAROM pain-free (no PROM), light theraband (ER/abduction/extension), biceps/triceps PREs, prone scapular retraction without weights, wall push-ups (no elbow flexion >90°), goal full ROM | Phase V (months 3-6): full ROM, light theraband with increasing resistance, light weight training at 4.5 months (no flies or pulldowns), regular push-ups, RTS at 6 months
Notes
Published 2024; Phase III AROM only — specifically prohibits PROM, AAROM, pulleys, supine wand, and wall climb (protects repair from passive tension); wall push-ups with no elbow flexion >90° in Phase IV; no flies or pulldowns until 4.5 months; light weight training at 4.5 months; RTS at 6 months post-op; stationary bike with arm in sling permitted Phase I; Kevin Roth MD Orthopedic Sports Medicine, Palo Alto CA (650-853-2943)
