Achilles Tendon Repair

Mammoth Orthopedic Institute / Sierra Park PT · Stephen Knecht MD (US Ski Team Physician)

Weight-Bearing
NWB x4 weeks in splint/cast; WBAT 4-6 weeks in CAM boot with heel lift; FWB weaning from CAM boot to supportive shoe wks 6-8
Key Restrictions
No over-stressing repair (forceful PF while in DF position); no impact activities until Phase V (3-6 months)
Timeline & Phases
5 phases: Phase I (0-4 wks, NWB splint/cast), Phase II (4-6 wks, WBAT CAM boot with heel lift), Phase III (6-8 wks, FWB wean from CAM boot), Phase IV (8-12 wks, no impact), Phase V (3-6 months, plyometrics and return to sport)
Notes
Edited by Stephen Knecht MD 9/23/19; Tim Tollefson PT initial edit 2/3/17; references University of Wisconsin Sports Medicine; return to sport criteria: dynamic neuromuscular control with multi-plane activities without pain or swelling
Surgeon / Author
Stephen Knecht MD (US Ski Team Physician)
Published
2019
