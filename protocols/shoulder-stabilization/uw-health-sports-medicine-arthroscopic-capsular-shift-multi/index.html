Shoulder Stabilization — Arthroscopic Capsular Shift (Multi-Directional Instability)

UW Health Sports Medicine · UW Health Sports Rehabilitation; UW Health Sports Medicine Physician group

Weight-Bearing
N/A (shoulder surgery)
Key Restrictions
Sling 6 weeks (more restrictive than Bankart); no shoulder IR past neutral weeks 1-6; no IR with abduction weeks 1-8; ROM progresses by week in Phase I; ER in ABD limited to ~60° in Phase II; no swimming until Phase V
Timeline & Phases
Phase I (0-6 wks): sling 6 wks, week-by-week ROM limits (no IR past neutral), isometrics from day 10; Phase II (6-12 wks): full ROM except ER in ABD ~60°, RC at 45°; Phase III (12 wks+): 5/5 RC at 90°, ER in ABD to 90°; Phase IV (15 wks+): high velocity, plyometrics; Phase V (20 wks+): sport-specific, RTS
Notes
For multi-directional shoulder instability. More restrictive than Bankart protocol: sling 6 weeks, no IR past neutral for 6 weeks, no IR with ABD for 8 weeks, ER in ABD limited to ~60° through Phase II. Week-by-week ROM progression in Phase I. Copyright 2018.
