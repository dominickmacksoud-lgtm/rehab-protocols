Shoulder Arthroplasty — Reverse Total Shoulder Arthroplasty

Orthopaedic Associates of Maine · Wolf

Weight-Bearing
FWB; ROM limits 0-6 wks: flex 0-150°, ER 30°, abd 90°
Key Restrictions
ROM limits 0-6 wks: flex 0-150°, ER 30°, abd 90°; no IR resistance until >6 wks; discharge to independent program when ROM plateau reached
Timeline & Phases
0-6 wks (PROM/AAROM within limits, isometrics except IR, begin Day 1-2), >6 wks (Thera-Band, scapular stabilization, add IR)
Notes
Dr. Wolf's protocol; PT begins Day 1-2 unlike Dr. Murray's 4-week hold; discharge when ROM plateau reached
Surgeon / Author
Wolf
