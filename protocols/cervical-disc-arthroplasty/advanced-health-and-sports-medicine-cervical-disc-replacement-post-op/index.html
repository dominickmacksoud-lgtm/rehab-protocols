Cervical Disc Arthroplasty — Cervical Disc Replacement Post-Op

Advanced Health and Sports Medicine Physiotherapy (AHSMP) · AHSMP Clinical Team (Sydney, Australia)

Weight-Bearing
WBAT; gentle walking <10 min Phase 1; progress to 20 min by wk 4
Key Restrictions
No neck bending/twisting x 2 wks; no lifting x 2 wks; log roll to get out of bed; avoid prolonged positions; lifting 10% BW wks 2-4, 15% wks 4-6, 20% wks 6-8; full ROM by wks 6-8; manual therapy to adjacent cervical segments only (avoid surgical segments)
Timeline & Phases
6+ weekly phases: wks 1-2, 2-4, 4-6, 6-8, 8-12, 12+; PT 1-2x/wk throughout
Notes
International (Australia); May 2023; Pilates reformer integrated from wk 2; 8-point plank and modified side plank criteria at wk 6-8; manual therapy to adjacent (not operated) cervical segments; occulomotor exercises Phase II+; virtually identical structure to AHSMP cervical laminectomy protocol; criterion-based weekly progression
Surgeon / Author
AHSMP Clinical Team (Sydney, Australia)
Published
May 2023
