Cartilage Restoration — MACI (Matrix-Induced Autologous Chondrocyte Implantation) — Patellofemoral Joint

Mammoth Orthopedic Institute / Sierra Park PT · Brian B. Gilmer MD (US Ski Team Physician); Karly M. Dawson PA-C

Weight-Bearing
TTWB x0-1 weeks; 25-50% WB x2-3 weeks; 75-full WB x4-6 weeks (may change if bipolar lesions); FWB weaning from crutches/brace at 6-12 weeks
Key Restrictions
Brace locked in extension for gait, unlocked for NWB ROM x6 weeks; ROM slow progression (0-20° wks 0-2, 30-60° wks 2-3, 90-130° wks 4-6); no OKC quad strength x6 weeks; CPM day 1 set 0-30°; no impact (running/jumping) x12 weeks; no cutting/pivoting/heavy impact x6 months; no running on uneven surfaces x9 months; RTS at 9 months
Timeline & Phases
3 phases: Phase I (0-6 wks, TTWB progressing to FWB, CPM day 1, ROM progression 0-130°), Phase II (6-12 wks, FWB, CKC double to single leg, balance/proprioception), Phase III (3-6 months, agility, single leg progression, running on controlled surfaces)
Notes
References MACI Rehabilitation Manual by Jay Ebert PhD AEP ESSAM (University of Western Australia); PF joint lesion — TTWB only x1 week (shorter than tibiofemoral variant which is TTWB x4 weeks); ROM progresses faster (0-20° wks 0-2 vs 0-30° wks 0-2 in tibiofemoral); RTS at 9 months; walk pain-free >3 miles all terrain for RTS
Surgeon / Author
Brian B. Gilmer MD (US Ski Team Physician); Karly M. Dawson PA-C
