Total Joint Replacement — Total Hip Arthroplasty (Anterior Approach)

Massachusetts General Hospital · MGH Sports Medicine

Weight-Bearing

4 phases: Phase I (0-3 days), Phase II (1-6 wks), Phase III (6-12 wks), Phase IV (12-16+ wks)
