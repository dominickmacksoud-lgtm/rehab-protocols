Fracture — Hip Fracture - ORIF

Dr. Jorge Chahla MD (Rush University Medical Center) · Dr. Jorge Chahla

Weight-Bearing
WBAT; no brace required
Key Restrictions
No treadmill (compression on new joint); no running/jogging, high impact aerobics, jumping rope, or plyometrics; no LE weight machines until 12+ wks; cuff weights up to 5 lbs at 7-12 wks
Timeline & Phases
4 phases: 0-3 wks, 4-6 wks, 7-12 wks, 12+ wks
Notes
Straightforward progression; emphasis on early walking (5 min/hr)
Surgeon / Author
Dr. Jorge Chahla
