Labral Repair — Arthroscopic SLAP Repair

Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center · OSU Sports Medicine

Weight-Bearing
Sling per surgeon; no lifting per surgeon guidance
Key Restrictions
ER in scapular plane: ≤15 deg wks 1-2, ≤30 deg wks 3-4, ≤50 deg wks 5-6, 90 deg wks 7-9; excessive ER above guideline is a precaution; excessive biceps loading is a precaution; no cross-body motion; no shoulder extension with elbow extension; review operative report before initiating rehab — includes reattachment, debridement, or combination; risk factors: overhead activity, FOOSH, contact sports, poor shoulder strength and scapular stability, faulty mechanics
Timeline & Phases
4 phases: Phase I Weeks 1-2 (PROM: elevation 75 deg scapular plane, ER ≤15 deg; scapular stabilization; submaximal isometrics — no elbow flexion), Weeks 3-4 (PROM: elevation 90 deg, ER ≤30 deg; AAROM begins), Weeks 5-6 (elevation 145 deg, ER ≤50 deg; AROM full can; IR/ER tubing), Phase II Weeks 7-9 (full flex/ABD, ER up to 90 deg), progressive strengthening and return to sport phases
Notes
.edu site; CPG format; SLAP type-specific surgical intervention noted (reattachment vs debridement vs combination — always review op report); risk factors table included; outcome tools: DASH, KJOC; isokinetic strength testing; staged ROM goals very detailed by week; overhead athlete and thrower considerations; discharge criteria: ROM per patient needs, full muscle strength/endurance, MCID on functional outcome
